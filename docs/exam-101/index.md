# Exam 101-500

The first of the two exams needed for LPIC-1. 60 questions, 90 minutes.

## Topics

| Topic | Objectives | Weight | What it covers |
|---|---|---|---|
| 101 System Architecture | 3 | 8 | Hardware, the boot process, runlevels and targets |
| 102 Installation and Package Management | 6 | 12 | Disk layout, bootloaders, libraries, apt, rpm |
| 103 GNU and Unix Commands | 8 | 26 | The shell, text processing, files, processes, regex, vi |
| 104 Devices, Filesystems, FHS | 6 | 14 | Partitions, mounting, permissions, links, the FHS |

**Topic 103 alone is 26 of the 60 points.** That is 43% of the exam in one
topic. If your study time is short, that is where it belongs.
